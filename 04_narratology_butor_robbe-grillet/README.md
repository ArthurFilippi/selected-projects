# Narratology of lived time in Butor and Robbe-Grillet

*Research paper, Department of Humanities and Social Sciences (HSS), École Polytechnique, supervised by Nicolas Wanlin.*

## Topic

A narratological study of the representation of **lived time** in the Nouveau Roman, based on *La Jalousie* (Robbe-Grillet) and *L'Emploi du temps* (Butor): how is the architecture of the narrative shaped, and at times broken, in order to render a subjective time, one that "resurfaces, dilates or is suspended"? The paper is organised in three movements: repetitions and "time travel", attempts at structuring, and the omnipresence of the present.
