# Invariants in knot theory

*Introduction to research in mathematics, Lycée Sainte-Geneviève (2022-2023). Collective work.*

## Topic

Construction and comparison of **algebraic and topological invariants** in knot theory. The aim is to classify knots up to ambient isotopy: in the absence of a complete invariant, one builds a family of invariants of increasing strength and proves the invariance of each one using the Reidemeister moves.

The paper covers the formalisation of a knot as an isotopy class and its reduction to diagrams, **tricolourability**, the **polynomial invariants** (Alexander, Conway, Kauffman bracket, Jones, HOMFLY-PT through skein relations), the **knot group** obtained from the fundamental group of the complement (Wirtinger presentation), then an outlook on **finite-type invariants** (Vassiliev) and on algorithmic aspects (Gauss codes).
