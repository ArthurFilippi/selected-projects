# On the use of deepfakes in modern information warfare

*Work carried out for **CIEDS** (Centre Interdisciplinaire d'Études pour la Défense et la Sécurité), as part of the Entente Cordiale Day Challenge, École Polytechnique.*

**Authors:** Arthur Filippi, Salvador Levy, Pierre Blusseau-Bourcier.

## Topic

The report (1) gives a **geostrategic state of the art** of the use of deepfakes in several recent conflicts (Russia-Ukraine, Israel-Hamas, India-Pakistan, Syria, sub-Saharan Africa); (2) presents their **generation** (GANs, diffusion, voice synthesis), **detection** and the countermeasures in place; (3) proposes **solutions**: *detect* (participatory CAPTCHAs, blockchain), *sanction* (non-proliferation, AI licensing), *educate*; (4) examines the shift towards **offensive use** by states.
